# Clinical Research Medicare Coverage & Billing Compliance

Prevent double billing while ensuring routine-care services and sponsor obligations are billed to the correct responsible party.

**Primary buyer:** Academic medical centers and research sites. **Evidence:** protocols, coverage analyses, budgets, informed consent, enrollment, orders, services, modifiers, claims, sponsor invoices, and payments.

Full local application built with React, Vite, Express, PostgreSQL, and OpenRouter. Includes 15 domain-specific capabilities, 105 custom AI workbench fields, three scenario-fill controls per feature, operational registers, workflow transitions, analytics, professional AI decision briefs, audit history, and at least 15 PostgreSQL records per capability.

## Domain capabilities

- Study protocol registry
- Coverage analysis
- National coverage rule mapping
- Sponsor budget mapping
- Participant enrollment
- Encounter order linking
- Routine cost determination
- Sponsor-paid service detection
- Q0 Q1 modifier control
- Diagnosis code validation
- Claim prebill review
- Sponsor invoice reconciliation
- Double-bill detection
- Correction refund workflow
- Study billing analytics
